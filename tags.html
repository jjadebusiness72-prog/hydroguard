Set up NFC tags

Each tag holds one web link. When someone taps their phone on the tag, that page opens. Write a resident’s profile link to a sticker on their wristband, and a door link to a sticker on their door reader.

How to write a tag

  1. Install the free NXP TagWriter or NFC Tools app on your phone.
  2. Below, press Copy link for the tag you want.
  3. In the app, choose Write, then Add a record, then URL.
  4. Paste the link and save the record.
  5. Press Write and hold the top of your phone flat on the tag until it says done.

Reading a tag

  • iPhone (XS and later): no app needed. Hold the top of the phone near the tag with the screen on, even on the lock screen. Tap the banner that appears.
  • Android: turn on NFC in Settings (search “NFC”). Hold the middle of the back of the phone near the tag.
  • No NFC? Point the phone camera at the QR code next to each link instead.
  • Which tags? NTAG213 or NTAG215 stickers, cards or wristbands. They’re cheap online and hold a link easily.

QR codes need an internet connection the first time this page loads. The links still work without them.
