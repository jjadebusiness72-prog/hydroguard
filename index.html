A wristband that notices first

HydroGuard is a soft wristband for residents in aged care homes. Every 2 hours it checks temperature, heart rate and hydration, so staff can catch early signs of a urinary tract infection (UTI) or dehydration before the resident becomes unwell. The same band is their room key and shows staff where they are.

  • Checks every 2 hours. Temperature, heart rate and a hydration index, scored 0 to 9.
  • Opens their door. Tap the band on the door reader. No keys to lose.
  • Shows where they are. Lounge, garden, dining room or their own room.
